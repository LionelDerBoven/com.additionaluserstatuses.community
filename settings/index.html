Which users count as "everyone"?

The "Everyone is at home" and "Everyone is asleep" Flow cards look at the users ticked below, live, every time a Flow runs. A new Homey user is counted automatically, so you never have to edit a Flow after someone joins the household. Untick anyone who should not hold the whole house back — a guest account, or a phone that never reports presence. Accounts disabled in Homey are always ignored.

Vacation

A user on vacation is left out of the "everyone" cards, so those keep working for whoever is still around. Vacation can also be set from a Flow, or from the user’s vacation device.
